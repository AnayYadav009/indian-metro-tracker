# Data license and attribution

## OpenStreetMap

The map's station and route geometry is derived in part from OpenStreetMap contributors. OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:

- License: <https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/>
- Attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors
- Attribution guidance: <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>

The pipeline retrieves OSM data through Overpass. Raw retrievals and generated datasets must retain their source metadata. A produced database or substantial extract must preserve the applicable ODbL notices and share-alike obligations.

## Other sources

Official operator, government, and reputable news sources are used for selected names, dates, status, counts, and verification notes. Their copyrights and terms remain with their respective publishers. Each value is accompanied by a source URL in the repository data.

Map tiles are provided by OpenFreeMap. Deployments must keep the visible map attribution supplied by the application and tile provider.

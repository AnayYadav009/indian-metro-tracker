## Assumptions & Design Choices

1. **Straight-Line Distance Labelling:** Per milestone specification, all computed distances are straight-line distances and are explicitly labelled as such (`... straight-line`) with metric units (`m` for $<1\text{ km}$, `km` with 1 decimal place for $\ge 1\text{ km}$).
2. **City Bounding Box Check for Zero-Operational Networks:** Rather than guessing from distant stations, when the user is located within a city's bounding box that currently has zero operational stations (e.g. Surat), the engine proactively informs the user that the city metro is under construction and provides the option to toggle upcoming stations.
3. **50 km Threshold:** Locations farther than 50 km from the nearest station display an informative message with the approximate distance to the nearest station and its city, preventing confusing results for remote locations.
4. **Client-Side Privacy:** All geolocation calls happen directly in the browser. Coordinates are held in React/Zustand component state in memory and discarded upon page reload/session end.

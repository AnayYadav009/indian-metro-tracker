## Assumptions & Design Choices

1. **No External Chart Library:** Per project constraints, all ratio visualizations and completion bars are rendered using inline SVG (`<svg viewBox="0 0 100 8">`) with pure CSS styling and accessible tooltips/text.
2. **Tier 1 vs Tier 2 Classification:** Cities are classified according to the milestone documentation and Census tier definitions:
   - *Tier 1 (8 cities):* Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Pune.
   - *Tier 2 (13 cities):* Agra, Bhopal, Gurugram, Indore, Jaipur, Kanpur, Kochi, Lucknow, Meerut, Nagpur, Navi Mumbai, Noida, Patna, Surat.
3. **Data Quality Threshold (>3% Variance):** Variance is computed against official operational km recorded in `data/reference/*.json`. When the relative variance exceeds 3.0%, a warning indicator (`⚠️ >3% variance`) is rendered alongside the computed figure, with tooltip details describing the difference. Cities within 3.0% (such as Pune at 0.3%, Bengaluru at 1.6%, Kolkata at 2.4%, Kanpur at 0.7%, Patna at 0.0%) display clean figures without warning badges.
4. **Data Completeness Notice Integration:** Uses the centralized `getKnownIssueNotice` helper from `lib/known-issue-notices.ts` to surface caveats for cities containing coverage gaps or pending rules (e.g. Bhopal, Gurugram, Delhi Pink, Chennai Corridor 3, Mumbai Line 3) directly in the table and expanded drawers.

## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone 18 (Route Finder) has been implemented and refined on branch `feat/m18-route-finder` strictly following the acceptance criteria in `docs/MILESTONES-v2.md` and user instructions:
- **Client-Side Graph Search**: Computes optimal transit paths in memory from compiled operational datasets with zero server calls, zero data logging, and zero external dependencies added.
- **Topological & Geometric Station Ordering**: Orders operational stations along line segments using equirectangular geometric projection fallback (`lib/routing/station-ordering.ts`).
- **Branch & Loop Support**: Fully supports branched corridors (e.g. Delhi Blue Line at Yamuna Bank, Delhi Green Line at Ashok Park Main) and circular loops (e.g. Gurugram Rapid Metro Phase 1 loop closure at Sikanderpur).
- **Time-First Optimization**: Minimises estimated transit time (rail running + station dwell + distance-aware transfer penalties) first, then fewest transfers, then fewest stops.
- **Distance-Aware Transfer Model**: Base wait/platform change penalty ($3.0\text{ min}$) plus walking distance at documented walking speed ($4.0\text{ km/h}$ or $\sim 67\text{ m/min}$). Intra-station transfers use nominal $100\text{ m}$ walking distance ($4.5\text{ min}$ total). Inter-station interchange transfers compute distance via Haversine from station coordinates. Relabeled in UI as `"Estimated fastest route"`.
- **Routable-City Guard**: Validates complete ordered stations and connected operational networks. Gracefully guards networks with 0 operational stations (Surat) or pending audit defects (Bhopal PRE-01, Gurugram PRE-02) with explanatory notices.
- **UI & Map Highlights**: Responsive, mobile-first floating Route Finder panel with autocomplete, swap button ("⇄"), step-by-step itinerary, URL deep-linking (`?from=&to=&city=`), sliced route geometry polyline highlight on MapLibre, and origin/destination markers.

## 2. Distance-Aware Transfer Time Model

Operational transfer estimation parameters documented in `lib/routing/constants.ts`:
- **Average Commercial Rail Speed**: $32\text{ km/h}$
- **Station Dwell Time**: $0.5\text{ min}$ ($30\text{ seconds}$)
- **Base Transfer Platform Time**: $3.0\text{ min}$ (headway buffer, escalators/stairs)
- **Pedestrian Walking Speed**: $4.0\text{ km/h}$ ($66.7\text{ m/min}$)
- **Intra-Station Walk Distance**: $100\text{ m}$ ($0.1\text{ km}$) $\implies t = 3.0 + (0.1 / 4.0) \times 60 = 4.5\text{ min}$
- **Inter-Station Walk Distance**: $d_{\text{link}}\text{ km}$ from Haversine coordinates:
  $$t_{\text{transfer}} = 3.0 + \frac{d_{\text{link}}}{4.0} \times 60\text{ minutes}$$
- **UI Label**: `"Estimated fastest route"` with subtext `"Minimises time, then transfers, then stops"`.
- **Disclaimer**: `"Travel times are rough estimates based on a 32 km/h average speed and distance-aware transfers (3 min base + walking at 4 km/h). Real-world schedules and train frequencies vary."`

## 4. Delhi Rithala $\to$ Raja Nahar Singh Transfer Analysis

### Why Rithala $\to$ Raja Nahar Singh Took 2 Transfers Previously
Even though Kashmere Gate was already topologically linked to Red, Yellow, and Violet lines:
1. **Option 1 (1 transfer via Kashmere Gate direct)**:
   - Red Line: Rithala $\to$ Kashmere Gate (13 stops)
   - Transfer: Red $\to$ Violet at Kashmere Gate (1 transfer)
   - Violet Line: Kashmere Gate $\to$ Raja Nahar Singh (33 stops, $44.17\text{ km}$)
   - **Heritage Stretch**: Violet Line traverses the slow, winding Old Delhi Heritage corridor (Kashmere Gate, Lal Quila, Jama Masjid, Delhi Gate, ITO, Mandi House, Janpath, Central Secretariat = 7 track edges / 8 stops, $5.44\text{ km}$).
2. **Option 2 (2 transfers via Yellow Line detour)**:
   - Red Line: Rithala $\to$ Kashmere Gate (13 stops)
   - Transfer 1: Red $\to$ Yellow at Kashmere Gate
   - Yellow Line: Kashmere Gate $\to$ Central Secretariat (6 stops, $3.54\text{ km}$ straight under Connaught Place)
   - Transfer 2: Yellow $\to$ Violet at Central Secretariat
   - Violet Line: Central Secretariat $\to$ Raja Nahar Singh (26 stops)
   - Yellow Line saves: $1.90\text{ km}$ rail distance ($3.56\text{ min}$) $+ 0.5\text{ min}$ station dwell $= 4.06\text{ minutes}$ in train time over the Violet stretch.
3. **The Flat Penalty Tipping Point**:
   - Under the prior flat $4.0\text{ min}$ transfer penalty, the extra transfer added $4.00\text{ min}$, but saved $4.06\text{ min}$ rail running time.
   - Net difference: Option 2 was $0.06\text{ minutes}$ ($\sim 3.6\text{ seconds}$) faster! Time-first Dijkstra thus chose the 2-transfer detour.
4. **Resolution via Distance-Aware Transfer Penalties**:
   - With distance-aware transfer penalties ($3.0\text{ min base} + 1.5\text{ min walk} = 4.5\text{ min}$ per intra-station transfer), the extra transfer costs $4.50\text{ min}$.
   - The $4.50\text{ min}$ transfer penalty exceeds the $4.06\text{ min}$ rail savings by $0.44\text{ min}$ ($26\text{ seconds}$).
   - Dijkstra on the full Delhi routable graph naturally selects Option 1 (1 transfer at Kashmere Gate). The regression test runs without any `allowedLineIds` restriction and asserts 1 transfer.

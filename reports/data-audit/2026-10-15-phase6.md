# Phase 6 — Construction status and missing geometry

Date: 2026-10-15  
Branch: `fix/m10-audit-errors`

## Scope and result

This phase checked the construction declarations requested for Delhi Pink, Kolkata Yellow, Ahmedabad Phase 2B, Pune Purple, Nagpur Phase II, and the remaining Mumbai Line 2B corridor. No new corridor geometry was added: the repo does not contain a source-backed alignment and terminal coordinates for the missing terminal stations on Pune/Nagpur, and the currently available official sources do not verify the exact Kolkata remainder alignment. Speculative geometry would violate the source and endpoint requirements.

One source-backed reference correction was made: DMRC's current network page records the Pink Line Maujpur-Babarpur–Majlis Park section as operational effective 2026-03-08. Delhi Pink reference operational figures now read 71.548 km and 45 stations; the former Phase 4 construction declaration is recorded as an opening. Source: https://delhimetrorail.com/pages/about-us/present-network and DMRC March 2026 network map: https://delhimetrorail.com/static/media/DMRC-Network-Map-Hindi-English-VA-23032026.00317293.pdf. The published station count delta from the previous reference (38 to 45) is 7.

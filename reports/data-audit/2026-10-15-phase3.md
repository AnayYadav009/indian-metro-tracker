## Delhi Blue — `del-blue-seg-01|del-blue-seg-02`

Audit reports ~525 m parallel overlap. Raw OSM relations are [447209](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/447209) (Noida Electronic City–Dwarka Sector 21) and [2535795](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/2535795) (Yamuna Bank–Vaishali). Their member station sequences share Yamuna Bank, but no common consecutive station pair. The shared-corridor schema requires at least two consecutive shared stations, so no declaration is justified by current evidence. The detected short parallel stretch may still be a physical track junction/approach, but that is not proven by these station sequences.

**NEEDS-OWNER:** confirm whether the ~525 m overlap near Yamuna Bank is a real shared rail section; if yes, provide an official route map or station-to-station limits. If not, provide the canonical source geometry for the affected segment(s).

## Delhi Green — `del-green-seg-01|del-green-seg-02`

The route sequences therefore do not establish an allowable shared corridor, and this audit does not suppress the finding based solely on a shared way ID.

## Noida Aqua — `noi-aqua-seg-01|noi-aqua-seg-02`

Audit reports ~624 m parallel overlap. Segment 1 uses [OSM relation 9268569](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/9268569). Segment 2 maps to [OSM way 538930428](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/538930428), tagged `railway=proposed`, `usage=branch`, named “Botanical Garden to Noida Sector 142 (Route-2)”. This identifies the suspect source as a proposed branch, not a depot spur. However, the compiled feature has three coordinates and two endpoint stations, while its cached OSM way has 53 geometry points and the available source does not expose an ordered station sequence for the segment. The 624 m overlap cannot be reliably classified as shared trunk versus duplicated/miscompiled geometry from current evidence.

**NEEDS-OWNER:** confirm whether the proposed Botanical Garden–Sector 142 branch intentionally shares the ~624 m segment with the Aqua Line; supply the official map/alignment and station-to-station limits, or identify the canonical way/geometry to use. The finding remains active pending that decision.

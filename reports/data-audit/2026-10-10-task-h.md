## Golden Line station list

The current [Delhi reference](../../data/reference/delhi.json) contains corridor
station counts and terminals but no authoritative station-name array. The
retrieved DMRC pages exposed only the generic official landing page and did
not publish a machine-readable Golden Line station list. The secondary source
used previously confirms the Silver/Gold/Golden naming history but is not an
official station-list source.

Consequently, no Golden Line station names or coordinates are proposed.

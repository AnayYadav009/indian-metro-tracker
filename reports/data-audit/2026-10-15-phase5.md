Mumbai’s compiled operational total is 86.0 km versus 90.49 km from the corrected reference rows (−5.0%). The pre-fix audit used 80.6 km versus 90.47 km (−10.9% using rounded fields; the audit’s full precision reported 10.8%). This initial total concealed the 5.4 km Line 2B segment because its override incorrectly said `construction` even though MMRDA reports two open phases. MMRDA lists 5.5 km / five stations operational from 2026-04-07 and a further 0.29 km / one station operational from 2026-08-13. I changed the segment and station statuses/dates using that source, rebuilt Mumbai from cached OSM, set the reference line total to the sourced stage sum 5.79 km and six stations, and removed stale accepted future-record warnings. Geometry was unchanged. The compiled 5.4 km is still 0.39 km below that staged total; no geometry was altered to match the figure.

The same MMRDA Line 9 overview gives Phase I (Dahisar East–Kashigaon) as 4.7 km and operational from 2026-04-07. The repo’s prior operational reference was 4.97 km, so I corrected it to the official 4.7 km and updated its source citation. The compiled geometry remains 3.5 km with four stations; **UNVERIFIED/data gap:** the repo does not establish why its geometry is 1.2 km shorter than MMRDA’s Phase I figure. Provide or approve a canonical source alignment before changing geometry.

The remaining Mumbai line differences (Lines 1, 2A, 7, and 3) are each 2.7–4.4%. Current checked MMRDA project material lists corridor lengths, but this phase did not establish whether the compiled-versus-reference differences are due to centerline measurement, route scope, or missing geometry. No geometries were changed. Line 2B’s remaining reference corridor, “Phase 2 (Chembur - DN Nagar),” has no matching compiled construction feature and remains an active `declared-not-compiled` error; it is a future construction gap, not part of the open 5.79 km.

Bhopal’s Orange Line is the only operational line in the repo. The reference says 6.22 km, while compiled geometry is 5.6 km; the audit still reports the 10.2% mismatch at full precision. PIB describes the Subhash Nagar–AIIMS priority corridor as approximately 7 km and lists eight stations, while MPMRCL’s operator fare chart also confirms those eight operational stations. Neither checked official source establishes an exact alternative to 6.22 km or supplies a geospatial trace. **NEEDS-OWNER / UNVERIFIED:** provide or approve the official Subhash Nagar–AIIMS alignment/chainage and confirm whether 6.22 km is the correct corridor measurement. I left both geometry and reference unchanged.

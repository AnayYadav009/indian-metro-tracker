### Classification Taxonomy
- **Class a (No geometry exists):** Station belongs to an unmapped line or an extension stretch where no segment geometry has been plotted (>2000 m from existing line geometry or line has no segments). Recommended fix: `add schematic connector`.
- **Class b (Wrong line):** Station is located directly along another line (≤200 m) but assigned to a distant line (>500 m). Recommended fix: `reassign`.
- **Class c (Geometry offset or wrong):** Segment exists for the assigned line in the vicinity, but polyline geometry is coarsely simplified, displaced, or missing intermediate track alignment (>300 m offset). Recommended fix: `fix geometry`.
- **Class d (Stale status):** Station represents a newly opened or under-construction extension whose segment data lacks updated status/phase attributes. Recommended fix: `update status`.
